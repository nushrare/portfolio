Hi, I’m Anushka.

I’m an interaction and UI/UX designer and developer from India, now based in Toronto. I’m also a model and a public speaker.

I came to interaction design through code. Modelling gave me a different way to think about what people notice first, and public speaking made me pay attention to what stays with them after that first impression. On a screen, I get to work with both. I care about how an experience feels when someone arrives, and whether they know what to do next.

Moving from India to Canada made me pay attention to the things you’re expected to figure out when you’re new somewhere. That’s part of why I made a data story about the cost of studying in Toronto. Seeing the same total in Canadian dollars and Indian rupees changes how I read it. I wanted visitors to be able to feel that shift for themselves.

Beyond the screen

I direct images for my modelling portfolio and enjoy speaking to a room as much as designing for someone on the other side of a screen. This site is also available in Hindi. I wanted there to be room for both languages in how I introduce myself.
