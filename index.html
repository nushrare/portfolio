About

Professional Summary

I’m a final-year Interaction Design student at George Brown Polytechnic with project experience in UX research, information architecture, user flows, wireframing, interface prototyping, and front-end development.

My prior Computer Science studies at Toronto Metropolitan University help me connect human-centred design with how interfaces are built. I’m interested in hands-on design and working closely with product and engineering teams to turn user needs into clear, accessible interfaces.
