About

Professional Summary

I’m an interaction and UI/UX designer and developer. My work spans UX research, information architecture, user flows, interface prototyping, and front-end development.

I turn user needs into clear, accessible interfaces and enjoy working closely with product and engineering teams to bring them to life.
