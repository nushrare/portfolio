01 / Web prototype

ANURAGA

A relationship support concept that brings together a daily check-in, attachment insights, and a communication toolkit.

Read the project story

ANURAGA organizes its home screen around three entry points: log how you feel, explore relationship patterns, or find scripts and repair tools for a difficult moment. The web prototype gives each a dedicated page, with a bottom navigation to move among them.

The check-in below is a small portfolio demonstration inspired by that flow.
