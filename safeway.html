SafeWay

Transit routes that account for safety and access.

My contribution

I co-designed the Figma wireframes and prototype. The team mapped how riders could choose a route and respond when conditions change.

The challenge

A fast route can be a poor fit when a rider is travelling after dark or an elevator is out. We explored how a transit app could show the information riders need before committing to a route.

How we designed it

Journey maps and a user flow helped us work through route selection, disruption alerts, and accessible rerouting. The flow offers a choice between the fastest route and Safe Mode, which considers lighting, crowding, and accessibility.

The prototype

The concept pairs visual and haptic alerts with an explanation of what changed and a suggested reroute. The prototype illustrates the interaction. Live transit feeds and safety criteria would need validation before launch.
